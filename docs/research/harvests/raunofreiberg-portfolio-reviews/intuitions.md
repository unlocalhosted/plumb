# Rauno Freiberg's intuitions, sorted by kind of seeing

From 40 portfolio reviews in one thread (2026-10-19), read against each page on 2026-11-26. Every point is in
`reviews.json` with what the page shows and how he got there; the numbers below (such as 21.3) are those points. This
file sorts them by the kind of seeing they need, and says what plumber has for each.

120 points: 37 about shape, 21 about craft detail, 41 about behaviour, 21 about content and story. The sorting is
ours. Where a point has two halves it is counted under the stronger one.

## His methods

These are his methods, and they matter more than any single point.

- **He watches his own hand.** He reached for zoom before reading (1.2), reached for a cube that would not turn
  (39.1), clicked a heading that looked like a link (21.3), moved the window to find a comfortable position (13.2).
  The hand reports the fault before he can name it.
- **He uses the thing as he really would.** He opens every project in a tab to compare them (18.1), presses each
  menu entry (1.4), reads an article to its end (42.1), finishes the game the page sets (41.6).
- **He compares a promise with what follows.** The share picture against the site (12.1). The look against the
  motion (36.1, 38.1). A hover against a click (40.4). An underline against a link (21.3).
- **He takes things away in his head** and keeps the page that is better (7.2, 25.1, 30.1).
- **He counts what each line or picture tells him** against the room it takes (24.1, 21.2, 13.1).
- **He draws the boxes.** For one page he outlined each block, wrote its size and proportion on it, and judged the
  arrangement of rectangles (40.1). This is the idea this whole line of work started from, done by hand.
- **He hands over the test and not the bug.** Set scrollbars to always show (8.1). Click back and forth fast (9.4).
- **He knows the field and the maker.** He knows what was in fashion and when (3.1, 3.3, 7.1), and who built the
  page (11.1).

## Content and story points

- **Lead with your strongest material.** Said five times (8.3, 14.2, 37.2, 40.3, 3.7), and its other half: choose two
  or three (27.1).
- **Say something.** A name is not a message (23.1, 10.1). Three lines that hold three facts (24.1). A headline and a
  sentence saying the same thing (35.2).
- **Keep people in your house.** Writing hosted elsewhere (32.1), projects that open other sites (31.3), a résumé on
  a file-sharing site (33.4).
- **The showing should match the size of the work** (17.2), and proof needs a name the reader knows (33.1).
- **Voice.** Third person as a pose (3.4). The patter of an advert (24.1).
- **Give a reason to take part** (12.4), and let an idea run through the whole page or drop it (41.5, 38.3).

Plumber has one check in this family, where a model reads a subtitle against its heading. The rest is advice about
the person's work, which a model could give and no measurement can.

## Before and after

Nineteen points were acted on by the site's owner between the review and our reading: 21.3, 1.2, 4.1, 7.4, 8.2, 8.3,
13.1, 13.2, 14.1, 14.2, 18.1, 25.1, 25.3, 25.4, 27.1, 30.1, 34.1, 37.1, 37.2. Each is a fault named by an expert and

Twenty-two points are unchanged today, and 53 were not checked, most of them because they need the live page.

## Six lessons for the engine

1. **"Looks like it means" against "means" holds up.** The largest group of shape points is alike against same role,
   and plumber can already read what it needs for it.
2. **The three-way judgement is his too.** Exact is fine, far apart is a choice, nearly is the fault. He says it in
   one line about one page (40.1) and applies it to proportion, edges, highlights and motifs.
3. **Loudness needs meaning.** Seven points turn on what matters most, which only the words can say.
4. **The page is wider than the page.** Tab icon, share picture, browser bars, and the other pages of the site. Ten
   points sit there, where plumber does not look.
5. **Conditions come first.** Thirteen ways of looking produced about thirty of the points. Several need no new
   check, only an old check run under a new condition.
6. **A third of it is behaviour over time.** No still reading of a page reaches those 41 points.

## Limits of this reading

- One reviewer, one evening, on portfolios. Portfolios are personal sites; a product screen is judged for other
  things too.
- The sorting into levels is ours, and so is every sentence under "how he got there". He wrote one line; we inferred
  the reasoning from the line and the page.
- For 19 sites the page he saw could not be recovered. Their pins are on today's page.
- 53 points were not checked against the live page.
