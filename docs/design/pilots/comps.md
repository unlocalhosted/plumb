## #16 Quarry, Today

- **Product.** Quarry, a time tracker for a one-person studio (Nadia Ferreira); calm, money on the line.
- **World.** A window with Quarry's sidebar (Today, This week 31h, Reports, Invoices 2) and the day's clients with their
  colours and hours; a toolbar with the week strip (hours under each day, Thursday marked).
- **Life.** The running timer ticks and its block on the day line crawls; five client colours carried from the
  sidebar dot to the day's blocks to each entry's bar; the day drawn as data (blocks, gaps, a 14:42 now line); an
  untracked 19 minutes shown as its own row with "Add entry"; the total against a goal ring (6h 08m of 7h).
- **Hierarchy.** Four levels: the running figure (34px), the entry titles and durations (13.5px medium), clients and
  times (12.5px muted), labels and axis (11px subtle).
- **Weak.** The running panel's violet wash is a little generic; the client marks are monograms where a real studio's
  clients would have logos. Both fine for a first comp.
- **Lesson.** Tabular figures: the running figure and the duration column are where proportional digits wobble.

## #59 Plate, Spring launch / Hero

- **Product.** Plate, a design tool; a three-person team working on a cabin-rental site (Stillwater).
- **World.** A toolbar (tools, the file, three collaborators' faces with the presenter ringed, Share, 100%), pages and
  a layer tree with type icons and nesting, the artboard, and a properties panel (X, Y, W, H from the card itself).
- **Life.** A real piece of design on the artboard: a photo, a kicker, a two-line headline, body copy, a pill CTA;
  the selection drawn in the tree's blue on the canvas with handles and its live size (250 × 365).
- **Composition.** The menus sit over the panel and empty canvas; the card reads in full beside them.
- **Weak.** The canvas is quiet around the artboard (no second frame, no comment pins); the tool icons are
  hand-drawn strokes, not a set. The lesson still sits on a context menu, and the menu is the most generic object.
- **Lesson.** The safe triangle: from Arrange, the straight way to Send to back crosses Hide.

## #57 Relay, Inbox

- **Product.** Relay, a team's mail client; mid-morning, twelve unread.
- **World.** Folders with counts, coloured labels, Compose; search with ⌘K; tabs with counts; the reading pane with
  its toolbar and a reply box.
- **Life.** Faces on every person; unread as a dot plus weight; label chips in the subject line; an attachment chip;
  the selected row tinted; the open message with two files, one a thumbnail of the Plate file (the worlds connect).
- **Density.** Seven rows of three lines in the list, as a desktop inbox shows them.
- **Weak.** Rows are uniform in height (no thread counts, no muted newsletters), and the reading pane's lower third is
  empty.
- **Lesson.** The @you in the selected row's preview: muted with opacity, it fades with its line.

## Against #16 as it stood

Each comp is busier with real things than the old #16 card and keeps its calm: a world, faces or colours, data drawn
as data, one running or selected state. None of them is a white card of text rows.
