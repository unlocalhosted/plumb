Low tide at Saltburn

Words by Nia Adebayo · Photographs by Owen Hale

The sea goes out so far here that the pier seems to stand on nothing. By seven the sand is ribbed and silver, and the dog walkers come down the cliff lift in pairs, coats zipped to the chin.

We walked the mile to the scar before the tide turned, and back with wet boots.

The moon up before dark, the water still going out. Photograph: Owen Hale
