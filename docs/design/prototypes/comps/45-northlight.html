Under the pink sign: the last neon workshop on Rua da Rosa

For forty years one studio has bent glass for the city’s bars. Then the landlord asked for the sign back.

Mei Lin3 November · Photographs by Ravi Nair

By the third night the studio had stopped arguing about the sign. Kaito Ren wanted it pink; the landlord wanted it gone; the bar downstairs wanted it on by ten, every night, whatever it said.

“Neon is a promise you make to the street at two in the morning.”

Kaito Ren, glass bender

The sign stayed. So did the crowd that gathers under it every Friday, most of whom have never been upstairs.

Ravi NairI was there at 1:40 and it was still on. It always is.
