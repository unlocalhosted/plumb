Your decisions are the tokens your theme defines and the reasons written beside them. Plumber doesn't ship a scale of its own. It reads yours, through your own Tailwind, and judges every value against it.

## Write the reason beside the token

Put a `/* why: … */` comment directly before a token, and Plumber keeps it with that step. When a value drifts, the steps Plumber offers come with the reasons you wrote, so whoever fixes it chooses by meaning rather than by the nearest number.

<DecisionSources />

A few things to know about reasons:

- **They're optional.** A token with no reason is still on your scale. Plumber just has nothing to say about why.
- **One sentence is kept.** A long reason is cut to its most relevant sentence, at most 100 characters.
- **A reason shared by more than three tokens** becomes a reason for the group, and isn't shown against each step. Write a reason where it tells one step from its neighbours.
- **Aliases don't inherit.** If `--color-primary` points at `--primary`, the reason on `--primary` stays there. Write it where the token is declared.

## Where decisions come from

Plumber collects tokens from these sources, in this order. When two define the same token, the later one wins.

1. **Presets** it finds in your project, such as shadcn's, or ones you configure.
2. **Your Tailwind v4 theme**, read through the Tailwind you have installed, with each token's place in your CSS.
3. **Variables on `:root` or `html`** in your CSS, for any family your config doesn't already cover.
4. **`decisions.<family>.from`** in `plumber.config.mjs`: theme selectors such as `'theme:--radius-*'`, CSS file globs, DTCG token JSON (where `$description` is the reason), or a function that returns the tokens.

Seven families are judged, each from its own namespaces:

| Family | Tokens |
| --- | --- |
| <Family id="spacing">Spacing</Family> | `--spacing-*` |
| <Family id="colour">Colour</Family> | `--color-*` |
| <Family id="radius">Radius</Family> | `--radius-*` |
| <Family id="type">Type</Family> | `--text-*`, `--font-*`, `--leading-*`, `--tracking-*` |
| <Family id="motion">Motion</Family> | `--duration-*`, `--ease-*`, `--animate-*` |
| <Family id="elevation">Elevation</Family> | `--shadow-*`, `--inset-shadow-*`, `--drop-shadow-*` |
| <Family id="z">Z-index</Family> | `--z-*` |

<Callout kind="careful" title="Tailwind's defaults stay unless you reset them">
Adding your own `--radius-lg` doesn't remove Tailwind's other radii. Reset a namespace in your theme with `--radius-*: initial;` so only your steps remain. Defaults that survive count as Tailwind's, not yours, and Plumber flags their use.
</Callout>

## See what Plumber read

```sh
npx plumber decisions                    # every token, its value, origin and reason
npx plumber decisions --family spacing   # one family
npx plumber decisions --json             # the same, for scripts
```

A token listed with `why: (none)` has no reason yet.

## When your decisions change

Once your project has a ledger, a new token, or a changed value, is an error in `plumber check` until someone accepts it:

```sh
npx plumber accept-decisions
```

That records the current decisions in the ledger, so a change to your scale is reviewed like any other change. Editing only a reason doesn't need it. A token that disappears is a warning; after reviewing the removal, `npx plumber ledger --prune` clears it.

<Callout kind="careful" title="--why applies to every decision, not just the new ones">
`accept-decisions` records all of your current decisions at once, and `--why` replaces the accepted reason on each of them. To add one token without rewriting the others' history, leave `--why` off: each token's own `why:` comment is used instead.
</Callout>
