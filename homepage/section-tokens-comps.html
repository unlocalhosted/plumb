A A 2px change: asking an agent for it, beside doing it on the page

Save tokens

A 2px change shouldn’t cost a prompt.

Describing a nudge to an agent means it reads your files, finds the class, edits, and you check again. On the page it’s three keys, and no model at all.

Your agent stops guessing.

When an agent does the fixing, Plumber hands it the file, the line and the class to write, with your theme’s reason for it. No searching your config, no reading files to find the value.
