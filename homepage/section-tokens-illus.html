A Two routes: one start, one finish, a long way round and a short way across

Save tokens

A 2px change shouldn’t cost a prompt.

Ask an agent and it goes the long way: through your files, your theme, an edit, and back to you to check. On the page it’s three keys and no model.

B The context: what one nudge puts in an agent’s context, beside the page’s

Save tokens

Your context is for building.

Every nudge you prompt fills the agent’s context with files it read to find one class. Nudge on the page instead, and the agent’s context stays on the feature.

C The loop: the prompt as a circle you go round twice; the page as a chord straight across it

Save tokens

Skip the loop.

A prompt goes round: you ask, it reads, it edits, you check, and again. On the page you go straight across: three keys, no model, the class written into the line.
