How plumber judges every form of the buy button

Each form of the button is used the way a person would use it: left alone, pointed at, pressed or held halfway, then finished. At each moment plumber’s whole engine runs on the live page and judges what is drawn right then, moving parts included.

How this is made

The forms are library buttons (Arc, beUI) redrawn in the product’s own look. A script drives each through its gesture in a browser and runs plumber’s engine in its “now” mode, which judges moving elements as they are drawn at that moment instead of waiting for them to settle. The notes are plumber’s own, in its own words. Built by generator/data/judge-show.mjs all.
