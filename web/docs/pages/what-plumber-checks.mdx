Plumber reads your page the way a person does (what is drawn, where it sits, how each part relates to the parts beside it) and reads your code for the values behind it. That is <CheckCount /> checks, grouped by what they are about.

Open any check to see what it catches and how Plumber fixes it. Where the fix moves a value, it plays here the way it plays on your page: the guides draw in, the value moves on the spring, the number lands on your theme's step.
